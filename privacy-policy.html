Chuck It: Bin Day Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Chuck It: Bin Day Android game ("the game") handles. The game is made by LeapYeet ("we", "us").

No account, no personal profile

The game has no sign-in and no account. We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your progress stays on your device

Your stars, scores, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, unlocked items and settings (sound, music, vibration and display options) are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. We don't upload them to any server. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your Google account backup, which is managed by Google.

Ads

The game can show optional rewarded ads, for example an extra throw or bonus coins in return for watching an ad. Ads are served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares data with Google, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, watched or tapped.
  • Diagnostics and performance data, such as crash and load-time information related to ads, plus basic device information (model, operating system version, language).

Data sent by Google's ads and consent SDKs is encrypted in transit (TLS). The game itself sends nothing to us. We don't receive this data in a way that identifies you. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings under Privacy > Ads.

Consent in the UK and EEA

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it opens, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), before any ads are requested. You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes. If you don't consent to personalised ads, any ads you choose to watch may be non-personalised or limited, or ads may not be shown at all.

To change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the game, go to Settings (from the main menu or the pause screen) and tap Privacy settings. The button appears wherever the law requires it. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and sensitive categories such as gambling, alcohol and dating are blocked. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help.

Ratings and reviews

After you've cleared a few levels, the game may show Google Play's own rating card once. Anything you enter there goes to Google Play, not to us.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

We run no servers and hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your progress is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Google keeps ad-related data under its own retention policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads and the consent form. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads.
  • Network state, wake lock and Android's ad services permissions: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads efficiently and use Android's privacy-preserving ad features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
