Chuck It: Bin Day

Chuck It: Bin Day is a landscape game for Android about getting rubbish into the bin. Pull back, let go, and land paper balls, cans and pizza boxes past wind, walls, fans, portals and seagulls across 20 levels and an endless mode. Every level hides a secret trick shot that pays coins for new trash, outfits, hats, bin skins and trails.
